Maintenance

Backup & Restore

Download every setting as one file, and upload it here or on another Chaos Router to restore it. The file contains passwords and VPN keys, so keep it somewhere safe. Unconfirmed changes are not included.

    Reboot

    Devices lose their connection for about a minute. Unconfirmed changes are reverted by the restart.

    Factory Reset

    Erases all settings, accounts, VPN keys and certificates, turns the router's services back to their defaults and reboots into the setup Wi-Fi. Download a backup first if you may want your settings back.
