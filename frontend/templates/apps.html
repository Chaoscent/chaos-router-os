            Apps Addon

            

                Run apps like Nextcloud, Jellyfin or Vaultwarden on the router,
                each reached by its own name in your network. The addon installs
                Docker Engine, Docker Compose and the App Manager. It takes a few
                minutes and needs internet. The router itself never depends on it.
