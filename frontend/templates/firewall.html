Firewall

Lets LAN devices reach SSH, this dashboard, DHCP, DNS and the internet. Nothing is opened towards the WAN. The rules follow the LAN/WAN setup automatically.

Routing & NAT

Forwarding lets the router pass traffic between the LAN and the internet. NAT shares the router's internet address with every LAN device and VPN client. Both are needed for LAN devices to get online. The internet interface is the modem, or eth0 when it is switched to WAN on the Network page.
