# Contributing to Chardsoft Router OS

Contributions are welcome: bug reports, ideas and code.

## License of contributions

Chardsoft Router OS is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Everything you contribute, for example as a pull request, comes under the same license (section 5 of the license): everyone, including the project, may use, change and share it, also in commercial products such as routers built on Chaos Router hardware.

By submitting a contribution you confirm that:

- it is your own work, or you have the right to submit it under the Apache License 2.0;
- if it contains someone else's work, you say so in the pull request, together with its license.

## How to contribute

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue with what you did, what you expected and what happened (and the version from the dashboard's sidebar).
- **Code:** branch from `dev/core` (or `dev/apps` for the Apps Addon), keep to the style of the surrounding code, and describe in the pull request what you tested and on which hardware.
