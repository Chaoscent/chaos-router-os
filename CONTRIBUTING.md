# Contributing to Chaos Router OS

Contributions are welcome: bug reports, ideas and code.

## Before your first pull request

Chaos Router OS is free for noncommercial use (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)). Its author also uses it in commercial products, such as routers built on the Chaos Router hardware, and may license it to companies. For that to stay possible with contributed code, every contribution comes under the agreement below.

**Add this line to your first pull request's description:**

> I have read the Contributor License Agreement in CONTRIBUTING.md and agree to it for this and all my future contributions to Chaos Router OS.

## How to contribute

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue with what you did, what you expected and what happened (and the version from the dashboard's sidebar).
- **Code:** branch from `dev/core` (or `dev/apps` for the Apps Addon), keep to the style of the surrounding code, and describe in the pull request what you tested and on which hardware.
